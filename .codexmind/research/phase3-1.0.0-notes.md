### 1.1 Schema-Driven Multi-Mod `.datc64` `DatPatchMerger` (`src/LocalExile.Client.AssetPipeline/`)
- **Bundled 3.29.3d `poe-dat-schema` (`data/schemas/poe-3.29-dat-schema.json` + MIT `LICENSE`):** Embedded resource in `LocalExile.Client.AssetPipeline.csproj` covering all **18 `AppendableRegistryTables`** plus `Stats`, `ItemClasses`, `WorldAreas`, `Words`, `Tags`, `FlavourText`, `SoundEffects`, and `UniqueStashLayout`.
- **`Dat64SchemaRegistry` (`Dat64/Dat64Schema.cs`):** Loads embedded 3.29 schema or external `schema.min.json`, enforces the 18 appendable vs. closed (`ItemClasses`, `Stats`) table boundaries, defines the **30 verified `DeadStatSlots`** (strictly excluding row `464` `dummy_stat_display_nothing`), and resolves columns by name, `__colN`, or `@offset`/`offset:N`.
- **`DatPatchDocument` (`Dat64/DatPatchDocument.cs`):** Declarative `.datpatch.json` schema & parser supporting:
  - `append` with `cloneFromRow` / `cloneFromId` and cross-table `$ref:TableName:EntryKey` wiring.
  - `modify` in-place updates by `rowIndex` or `id` (e.g., `StackSize = 65535`, safe `Mods` / `Stats` non-ID edits; protects `Stats.Id` and `Stats.HASH32`).
  - `allocateDeadStat` over the 30-slot dead `Stats` pool, preserving each row's stock `Stats[row].Id` & `HASH32` (zero binary hooks, passes both `Game.exe` and LocalExile `Stat.Checksum`) and emitting UTF-16LE BOM+CRLF `Metadata/StatDescriptions/<mod>_custom_stats.txt` overlays.
- **`Dat64Table` & `Dat64TablePatcher`:** Full typed readers/writers (`bool`, `i16`, `u16`, `i32`, `u32`, `f32`, `string`, `row`, `foreignrow`, `enumrow`, and `[]` variants), `UpdateRow`, `CreateBlankRow`, `AppendStringFresh`, and zero-aliasing `CloneRowDeep` deep-copying every variable-section array/string slice (`VerifyNoPointerArrayAliasing`).
- **`DatPatchMerger` + `merge-dat-patches` CLI (`Dat64/DatPatchMerger.cs`, `Program.cs`):** Kahn topological + ordinal `modId` deterministic merge order, two-pass row reservation & cross-table `$ref` resolution (including `$ref:Stats:<deadStatKey>`), automatic `HASH32` (`PoeHashing.Hash32`), collision-free non-zero `HASH16`, unique `MtxTypes.__col18`, hard rejection of closed-table appends, `> 65,535` row ceiling, and `> 30` dead-stat exhaustion, emitting `Mods/Client/.merged-cache/` + deterministic `content-row-map.json` + SHA-256 handshake `digest`.
- **Verification:** `LocalExile.Client.AssetPipeline.Tests` **48/48 PASS** plus live smoke merge against real 3.29.3d `/home/nam/Git/LocalExile/Client/Bundles2` across `BaseItemTypes`, `CurrencyItems`, `ItemVisualIdentity`, `Mods`, `MtxTypes`, `Pet`, and `Stats` (row `99`, `0` stride drift, `0` aliasing).
